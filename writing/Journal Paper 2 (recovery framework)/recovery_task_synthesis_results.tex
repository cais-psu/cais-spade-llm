\subsubsection{Recovery Task Synthesis Results}

Across the four failure scenarios in Fig.~11, the neuro-symbolic framework
produced a passing recovery outline in 230 of 240 attempts (95.8\%), compared
with 70 of 240 (29.2\%) for the Informed baseline and 10 of 240 (4.2\%) for
the Baseline. Each bar represents 30 attempts, ten per \gls{llm}. Every
completed outline was checked for feasibility, safety, and task continuation.
Only the framework used feedback from the \glspl{ra} and the \gls{cca} to
revise proposals during synthesis. Its remaining ten attempts ended without
an outline.

Adding the safety specifications to the prompt reduced unsafe outcomes from
144 of 240 Baseline attempts (60.0\%) to 30 of 240 Informed baseline attempts
(12.5\%). The Informed baseline produced 44 passing outlines in 60 Part
slippage attempts, compared with 26 in 180 attempts across the three breakdown
scenarios. Across all four scenarios, 63 Informed baseline outlines failed a
symbolic or physical feasibility check. A further 58 passed the event checks
but left the cell in a state from which the remaining nominal tasks could not
be completed.

The framework produced 112 passing outlines in 120 attempts with \gls{ol}
generation and 118 in 120 with \gls{el} generation. All six additional passes
came from Conveyor breakdown, where the count rose from 23 to 29 of 30. For
UR5e-1 robot breakdown, 29 of 30 attempts passed at each level. For M1
machine breakdown and Part slippage, all 30 attempts per scenario passed at
each level.\footnote{For Part slippage,
Fig.~11(b) reports the full event-level framework rerun after the Exit model
was corrected to represent the removal of a part. Before the correction, 18
of 30 attempts passed.}

The framework used more tokens at \gls{el} in all four scenarios when
measured by the median per attempt. For Conveyor breakdown, median generation
time was similar at the two levels (5.8~min at \gls{ol} and 5.7~min at
\gls{el}). M1 machine breakdown showed the largest increase in both token
use and generation time. The medians rose from 82,000 to 270,000 tokens per
attempt and from 5.1 to 9.6~min, while median lookups fell from 18 to 16.
