\newacronym{llm}{LLM}{Large Language Model}

\newacronym{ol}{OL}{outline level}
\newacronym{el}{EL}{event level}

This supplement describes the prompts that the neuro-symbolic framework sends to the~\gls{llm}.  In recovery task synthesis, the~\gls{pa} prompts the~\gls{llm} to propose ranked candidates for the next recovery event at~\gls{el}, or a complete recovery outline at~\gls{ol}. At both levels, the~\gls{ra} checks the feasibility of each proposed event,
and the~\gls{cca} checks its safety (\FigOutline{} of the paper). In primitive composition, the~\gls{ra} prompts the~\gls{llm} to compose each accepted event from the primitives of the resource (\FigComposition{} of the paper). Appendix~A describes the structure of the recovery task synthesis prompt, and Appendix~B describes the structure of the primitive composition prompt. Each appendix includes an illustrative walkthrough from the Part slippage scenario.\footnote{The gray boxes reproduce recorded text. Minor implementation details are omitted and marked [...].} Appendix~C presents the recoveries for the four failure scenarios and their execution results in Gazebo.
